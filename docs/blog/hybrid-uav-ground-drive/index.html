Commissioning the ground-drive stage of a hybrid aircraft

The wheeled-aircraft project required its own motor-control and safety baseline.

Achraf RAHIBE · 28 October 2026 · Project retrospective

Project photo to be added

The challenge: separate the two operating modes

An aircraft that can drive on wheels brings different control paths into one platform. This engagement focused on the ground unit before the later flight-and-drive merge. It is a separate project from the drone landing on a rover.

Make ground commands explicit

I integrated the CUAV X7+ ArduRover baseline, dual VESC motor paths, skid-steer mixing and RC safety gating. The work connected the operator commands and autopilot configuration to the wheel-drive interfaces.

The delivered milestone

I documented the ground-control behaviour, setup and next integration steps in a commissioning report. The ground milestone is the completed delivery shown here. Combining wheel operation with flight was a later stage, so I do not present it as a finished hybrid-flight result.
