Giving a Micro Talon vertical takeoff capability

The conversion linked new lift hardware to the fixed-wing aircraft's existing cruise role.

Achraf RAHIBE · 28 October 2026 · Project retrospective

The challenge: add lift without losing the aircraft

Converting a fixed-wing airframe into a QuadPlane changes its mechanical and electrical interfaces. Four lift motors need mounting, power and control, while forward propulsion and the original flight surfaces remain part of the aircraft.

Build, configure and check the handoff

I produced mounting parts, integrated the lift propulsion and electronics, and configured the SpeedyBee F405 WING Mini with ArduPlane. Calibration, hover modes, transition parameters and mission setup were part of the same integration sequence.

The recorded result

The project includes a physical VTOL conversion and a documented flight-test sequence, including an initial QStabilize takeoff and landing. That is the validation I show. Wider transition reliability is a separate question and is not inferred from a single successful takeoff.

Project record

The published evidence includes an initial QStabilize takeoff and landing and mission-review material. Full operational transition reliability is not quantified.
