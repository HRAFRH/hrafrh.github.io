Building a 10-inch FPV aircraft for assisted flight and missions

Propulsion, power, flight controls and failsafes were developed as a complete aircraft.

Achraf RAHIBE · 28 October 2026 · Project retrospective

The challenge: go beyond a frame and motors

The 6S quadcopter needed to support manual handling, assisted flight and waypoint missions. The component choices and electrical integration therefore had to serve both the aircraft's propulsion demands and its autopilot functions.

Connect hardware to behaviour

I selected propulsion and power components, integrated Pixhawk 6X and sensors, and completed wiring and calibration. I then worked on flight modes, mission setup, failsafes and PID tuning. This joined the bench integration to the behaviour that would be checked in the field.

What was tested

The documented work includes assisted-flight and mission testing, plus approximately 2 kg payload testing for this development configuration. Bench and field images show the aircraft at different stages. Those checks belong to this build and are not a payload guarantee for every 10-inch aircraft.
