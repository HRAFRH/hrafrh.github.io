Connecting perception to a usable vehicle payload

Detection, camera control and video transport belong to the same integration problem.

Achraf RAHIBE · 28 October 2026 · Project retrospective

The challenge: make the output useful to the vehicle and operator

A detection model by itself is not a complete payload. The camera stream, companion computer, tracking workflow and gimbal or platform interface all have to connect. My work covered those interfaces across embedded vehicle applications.

Join compute, camera and transport

I integrated Jetson and Raspberry Pi companion computing, YOLOv8 and ByteTrack workflows, and camera or gimbal interfaces with RTSP and GStreamer video transport. On the USV work, Jetson Nano and an A8C gimbal connected first-person video and AI-detection workflows with operator visibility.

The delivered work

The contribution was integration of perception, video and platform interfaces. The project is presented as an embedded payload workflow rather than an unsupported accuracy or autonomy claim. Its supporting images provide the hardware context, with room for more application-specific material.
