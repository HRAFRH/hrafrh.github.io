The Ranger held level, but kept turning right

Working through a fixed-wing yaw problem with the client, one configuration check at a time.

Achraf RAHIBE · Project retrospective

Start with the behaviour

The Volantex Ranger was a pusher fixed wing running INAV. The client reported that it held level in stabilised modes but continued yawing to the right, with left rudder needed to counter the turn. Keeping the wings level had not solved the heading problem.

Separate the possible causes

I reviewed the configuration rather than treating every turn as a PID problem. The report identifies board-alignment offsets, the magnetometer configuration and strong yaw feed-forward as points to investigate. Rudder direction, control authority and the pusher thrust line were also part of the diagnosis. These were hypotheses to test, not five independently proven faults.

Make the next test useful

I prepared an ordered set of checks so the client could isolate one influence at a time. A separate Blackbox guide explained how to prepare the recorder and retrieve the next log. The two documents are visible below, alongside the reasoning they supported. Their settings belong to this aircraft and are not a universal fixed-wing configuration.

Keep the outcome tied to the evidence

I helped the client resolve the yaw issue. The documents shown here preserve the investigation and support work; they do not include a final post-fix flight log. Matching client photographs and flight recordings will be added when available, so another aircraft is not used as a substitute.
