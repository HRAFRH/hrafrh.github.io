My contribution

Reviewed Pixhawk 6C / ArduPlane configuration, motor and servo assignments, compass and airspeed interfaces, calibration and failsafes. Analysed supplied flight logs and recordings, attitude response, vibration, clipping and FFT spectra, then documented filter and PID-tuning recommendations.

Delivered work

An engineering report, vibration and notch-filter analyses, configuration guidance and iterative remote flight-test support. The media library follows the original test sequence rather than presenting every recording as a final acceptance test.

Scope and validation

Scope: configuration, diagnostic analysis and remote client test support. The recordings span field trials and ground tests at different stages of the work; individual clips do not establish final aircraft certification or an unrestricted flight release.
