Flying-wing VTOL configuration rebuild and flight testing

The client brought me an unstable 4+1 flying wing. The initial review revealed several missing or incomplete configuration items, so I took responsibility for rebuilding the flight-control setup from scratch.

Complete configuration rebuild followed by stable client flights

The client’s silver flying-wing QuadPlane in the workshop.

My contribution

I led the engineering work from diagnosis through flight testing. I rebuilt the Pixhawk 6C and ArduPlane QuadPlane setup, covering motor and servo outputs, sensor interfaces, calibration, flight modes and failsafes. During live calls, I guided the on-site team through checks and reviewed the recordings and flight logs. Attitude tracking, IMU clipping, vibration and FFT data informed changes to the filters and PID tuning.

Delivered work

The client team achieved stable flights after the rebuild and iterative tuning. I delivered the rebuilt configuration, a test and troubleshooting workflow, and engineering reports covering the setup and analysis.

Scope and validation

I led configuration, analysis and remote engineering support. The client team operated the aircraft on site. The video gallery includes different stages of testing, including earlier handling checks and follow-up hover footage.
