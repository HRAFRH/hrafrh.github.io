My contribution

Developed and tested the combined UAV-rover system, integrating aircraft and rover autopilots, CUAV X7, dual VESC motor control, MAVLink communication and coordinated docking. Worked on actuator synchronization, isolated power, autopilot handshakes and failsafe checks.

Delivered work

An integrated, tested drone-rover platform, with the control, communications and docking subsystems developed together.

Scope and validation

Owner-confirmed end-to-end development and system testing. The four-actuator docking mechanism and its staged integration are documented separately within the system work.
