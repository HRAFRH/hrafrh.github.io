My contribution

Simulated many of the aircraft I later flew, checking component interfaces, autopilot behaviour, flight modes, mission workflows and parameters before field tests. I also converted the Cobra VTOL aircraft files into a custom Gazebo model and connected it to PX4 and ArduPilot, rather than relying only on pre-built simulator aircraft.

Delivered work

Gazebo model integration, autopilot SITL connections and repeatable pre-field checks across multirotor, fixed-wing and VTOL configurations.
