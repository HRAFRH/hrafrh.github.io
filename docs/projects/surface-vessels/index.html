01Diesel rescue-vessel autonomy systemEngineering development and test work+

Develop the electrical and autonomy architecture for converting a diesel-powered rescue boat into an uncrewed surface vessel.

My responsibilities

Prepared the system architecture and BOM, rudder and throttle interfaces, CUAV V6X / ArduRover configuration planning, and power, safety, navigation and compute integration. Specified RTK, AIS, NMEA2000, companion compute and communications interfaces.

Delivery

System and procurement documentation with integration support for engine controls, power and safety equipment, and navigation electronics.

Evidence and validation

A completed client engagement and project photographs support the design and integration scope. No independently measured sea-trial performance, certified autonomy level or rescue deployment is asserted.
