02Unmanned surface vehicle developmentFull-system development and water testing+

Develop a 4 m twin-waterjet unmanned surface vehicle, from system architecture through navigation, perception and water trials.

My responsibilities

Developed the system from architecture and component selection through electrical integration and water testing. Integrated autopilot navigation, differential-thrust waterjet control, ESC and battery/BMS interfaces, and investigated pre-charge/main-contactor switching. Integrated FPV and AI-detection workflows using Jetson Nano and an A8C gimbal camera.

Delivery

An integrated USV platform with propulsion control, autopilot navigation, onboard camera/perception interfaces and water-test work.
