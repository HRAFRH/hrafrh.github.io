About Me

Hello, I'm Achraf RAHIBE, a UAV and avionics systems engineer based in Rabat, Morocco. My work focuses on the electronics, flight controls and interfaces that bring uncrewed aircraft, ground robots and surface vessels together.

I coordinate electronics integration across embedded, RF and mechanical systems, configure autopilots, investigate stability issues and prepare technical reports. My work brings together system design, hands-on troubleshooting and clear documentation.

I work from the problem through configuration, bench checks and log review, documenting what changed and what still needs to be validated. My project library also includes airframe design studies, fabrication preparation and surface-vessel system planning.

I also work with Photoshop for photo editing and Illustrator for vector graphics, bringing clear visual communication into engineering documentation.
